What we offer

Stuck on a course? LockdIn pairs you with a tutor who fits the way you learn, not just what you study. Our tutors are top students who already passed the same courses you are taking, so they know where students get stuck and what the exam will ask.

On top of one-on-one sessions, you get study notes, past exams with full solutions and AI-generated practice questions built around your courses.

From high school to university, we cover any course our tutors have mastered, including:

  • Math and statistics
  • Sciences: physics, chemistry and biology
  • Programming and engineering
  • Business and economics
  • Languages and writing

Don't see your course? Tell us and we will find a tutor for it.

Our partners and how we operate

We work with high schools, student associations and libraries to reach the students who need help the most.

Every new student fills out a short questionnaire. LockdIn uses the answers to match them with the right tutor, then they book their sessions online or in person.

After each session, the student rates the tutor so we can keep the quality of our tutoring high.

Our goals for our first year

  • 500+ students helped
  • 4.8/5 average tutor rating
  • 3 in 4 students raise their grade in one month
  • 1000+ AI practice questions
