Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. The paid plan is month to month, so you can cancel whenever you want and keep your access until the end of the month.

How do I book a session with my tutor?

After you fill out the questionnaire, we match you with a tutor. You can then book your sessions online or in person from your account.

Which subjects can I get help with?

Any high school or university course our tutors have mastered, including math, sciences, programming, business and languages. Don't see your course? Tell us and we will find a tutor for it.

What is included in the free plan?

You can explore LockdIn, see our tutors and try a few sample questions. Upgrade to get unlimited notes, AI quizzes and one-on-one tutoring.
